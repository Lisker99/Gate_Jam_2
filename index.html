Gate Jam Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

Gate Jam ("the app", "we") is a puzzle game for Android published by Lisker. This policy explains what information the app handles. Contact: LiskerGoIrish@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Gate Jam has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics or tracking tools of our own.
  • The game runs offline. Your progress is saved only on your device.
  • If you choose to watch an optional rewarded ad, Google's AdMob service collects some device information to show it. See "Ads" below.
  • If you buy the Supporter Pack, Google Play handles the payment. We never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your progress (current level, unlocked levels, stars, settings such as vibration, which tips you have seen, the day you last used a free daily revive, and whether you own the Supporter Pack) in a file on your device. This file is not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it. Backups made by Android or Google Play may include it, according to your device's backup settings.

Ads

Gate Jam shows only rewarded ads, and only when you tap a button to watch one in return for a bonus. We do not show banner, interstitial or app-open ads.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When you choose to watch an ad, AdMob and its partners may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and ad interaction data, to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. This data is collected by Google, not by us. Learn more at policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region that requires it, the app shows a consent form (provided by Google's User Messaging Platform) before any ad data is used. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Purchases

The app offers one optional one-time purchase, the Supporter Pack. Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We receive only confirmation from Google Play that the purchase was made, so the app can unlock it and restore it if you reinstall. We do not receive or store your name, card number or other payment details.

Children

Gate Jam is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through an ad or purchase, contact us and we will help.

Sharing

We do not sell your information and do not share it with anyone, because we do not collect any. The only third parties involved are Google (AdMob for ads, Google Play for purchases and distribution), which handle data under their own policies.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over data held about you, such as access, correction and deletion. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to provide or delete. To remove local game data, clear the app's storage or uninstall it. For data held by Google, use your Google Account's privacy controls.

Security

The app does not transmit your game data. Ads and purchases use Google's services, which use encrypted connections.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, mention it in the app's update notes.
